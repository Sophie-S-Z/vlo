Ladies and gentlemen, distinguished faculty, proud families, and most importantly, the graduating class of 2025—good evening.

Four years ago, many of us arrived on this campus unsure of who we were or what we wanted to become. We were nervous, excited, and maybe a little overwhelmed. I remember standing in line at orientation, clutching a map I couldn't read, wondering if I had made a terrible mistake.

But somewhere along the way, this place became home. We found friends who stayed up with us through late-night study sessions. We found professors who challenged us to think more deeply and believe in ourselves more fully. We stumbled, we failed, and we learned that failure is not the opposite of success—it is part of it.

As we step into the next chapter of our lives, the world we are entering is uncertain. We face challenges that previous generations could hardly have imagined. But if these four years have taught us anything, it is that we are capable of far more than we think. We are resilient. We are curious. And we are ready.

So tonight, let us celebrate how far we have come. Let us thank the people who helped us get here. And tomorrow, let us go out into the world with courage, compassion, and the determination to make it a little better than we found it.

Congratulations, Class of 2025. We did it.
